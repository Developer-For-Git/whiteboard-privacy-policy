Privacy Policy

App: Whiteboard: Draw, Write, Notes | Last updated: November 25, 2026

Key Summary: Whiteboard is an offline-first application. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, drawing content, sketches, audio recordings, or files. Everything you create stays strictly on your personal device.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy describes how Orbiton Studio ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information in the Whiteboard: Draw, Write, Notes mobile application (the "App"). We are strongly committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your creative workspace.

2. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use the App.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or physical location.
  • No Board or Drawing Data: All boards, notes, drawings, annotations, imported PDFs, and math equations are stored locally on your device's internal storage and are never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary for user-initiated features:

  • Camera: Used exclusively when you choose to take a photo of a physical blackboard, textbook, or document to insert onto your canvas, or when using the optional Face Cam feature during screen recording. Photos are processed locally.
  • Microphone: Used only when you explicitly choose to record a lesson using the built-in Screen Recorder with voice narration. Audio is saved directly into the MP4 video on your local device.
  • Storage & Photos / Media: Used solely to allow you to select images from your gallery to insert onto your canvas, and to save your exported high-resolution PNG, JPEG, and PDF documents to your device.
  • Foreground Service: Used strictly to ensure smooth, uninterrupted screen recording when you activate the recording studio.

4. On-Device Artificial Intelligence (ML Kit)

The App uses Google ML Kit (Digital Ink Recognition and Text Recognition) for handwriting-to-text conversion and OCR. All recognition algorithms run 100% on-device. Your strokes, handwriting, and text images are processed locally within your device memory and are never transmitted across the network.

5. Advertising & Analytics

No Ads & No Tracking: The App contains no third-party advertisements, no behavioral tracking pixels, and no user-tracking analytics SDKs. Your creative process is completely uninterrupted and private.

6. Children’s Privacy & Educational Safety

The App is designed as a neutral educational and productivity tool suitable for users of all ages, including students, teachers, tutors, and children. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, the App fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy standards.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device, you maintain complete control:

  • You can delete individual boards, drawings, or pages anytime from the in-app gallery.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through Android Settings permanently removes all local data and files created within the App.
